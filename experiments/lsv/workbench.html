LSV Phase A format workbench

Research tool for explicitly selected local files. Selecting a file reads its header and manifest. Choose a listed member and press Read to load and decompress that member. Resource output shows structural counters.

Limits: 128 MiB file/member; 512 manifest entries; 512 MiB cumulative expansion; 1 MiB strings; depth 64; 30 seconds cumulative active worker execution; 10 MiB report. These limits are provisional.

Select one local .lsv file to begin.
